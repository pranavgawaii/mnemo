02 · Colour

One canvas, one ink, one accent.

Everything else is a tint or a semantic signal. The accent should cover about 5% of the page; if a screenshot looks purple, you have gone too far.

Click a swatch to copy its hex. Contrast ratios and semantic rules: see docs/03-colors.md.

05 · Motion

Reveal once. Then settle.

A 14px fade-up over 0.7s with a strong ease-out. It fires at 12% visibility, once. Buttons lift 1px; the marquee drifts and pauses on hover. Everything stops under reduced motion.

14px

The reveal distance. A settle, not a slide.

0.7s

A slow ease-out that makes the page feel expensive.

Once

The observer unobserves after the first entry.
